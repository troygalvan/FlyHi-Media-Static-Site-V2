Why it matters

People google you first.

Your face is your brand

Clients look you up before they ever call. Your headshot is on Zillow, Google, LinkedIn, and every card you hand out — it's doing sales work whether it's good or not.

Consistency sells the team

A roster of mismatched selfies and decade-old portraits undercuts the brand. One shoot puts the whole team in the same light, on the same background, at the same standard.

Old photos cost trust

When the person who walks in doesn't match the photo, the meeting starts on the wrong foot. A current headshot keeps the first impression honest — and working for you.
