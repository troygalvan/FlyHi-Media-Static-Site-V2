Why it matters

Commercial listings compete too.

Leasing starts online

Tenants and their brokers shortlist from LoopNet, Crexi, and your marketing package before anyone tours. Strong media gets your property on that shortlist; phone photos keep it off.

Assets need presentation

Investor decks, lender packages, and annual reports all need current, professional imagery of the asset. One shoot covers marketing, reporting, and the file you'll need next quarter.

Aerials tell the site story

Access, parking, traffic counts, surrounding development — the things commercial buyers and tenants actually evaluate are best shown from above. Drone coverage turns a spec sheet into a site story.
