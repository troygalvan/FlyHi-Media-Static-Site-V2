Why it matters

Your listing competes in a grid.

The cover photo is the ad

Guests see your listing as one tile among twenty. The cover photo decides who clicks — and platforms rank listings that get clicked higher, so a stronger photo compounds into more visibility.

Guests book the experience

Nobody books a floor plan — they book the hot tub, the game room, the coffee bar, the fire pit. We shoot amenities the way guests imagine using them, because that's what earns the reservation.

Better photos hold higher rates

A listing that looks premium gets treated as premium. Professional photography supports higher nightly rates and fewer calendar gaps — it pays for itself in a booking or two.

What we shoot

A shot list built for hosts, not listings.

Amenity Coverage

Pool, hot tub, outdoor kitchen, game room, workspace — every amenity guests filter and search for gets its own hero shot, not a corner of a wide angle.

Lifestyle Details

The coffee setup, the stocked bar, the styled patio table — detail shots that make guests picture their stay instead of inspecting your square footage.

Twilight Exteriors

Dusk shots with the lights on are the highest-clicking cover photos on Airbnb. If your property has outdoor living or a pool, twilight is the money shot.

Drone & Location Context

Aerial shots that show the property, the land, and what's nearby — because "minutes from the River Walk" lands harder when guests can see it.
